# Editing Rules — Digital Marketing Strategy Branch

**Branch:** `work/digital-marketing-strategy` only  

---

1. **Edit marketing strategy and campaign files only.**  
   Prefer `handoff/grok/growth/`, `handoff/grok/campaign/`, `handoff/grok/blog/`, `seller-pack/drafts/`, and `digital-marketing-strategy/`.

2. **Do not edit website source.**  
   Off-limits: `index.html`, `app.js`, `planner.js`, `style.css`, `site-config.js` (product config for the demo site), and other site runtime files unless an explicit separate task says otherwise.

3. **Do not change the Renovation Scope & Budget Planner** implementation or behaviour in code.

4. **Do not change Etsy integration code** or Francis Listing Manager automation from this marketing task.

5. **Do not publish content.**  
   No Etsy publish, no social scheduling, no community posting from the agent unless the human explicitly does it outside this workflow.

6. **Do not create fake screenshots.**  
   Use real final product captures where media is required.

7. **Do not invent ratings, reviews, customers, results, or sales.**

8. **Do not claim guaranteed leads or guaranteed estimates.**  
   Budget guidance is preliminary only — not a binding quotation.

9. **Do not create fake scarcity** (false countdowns, fake “only 3 left”, etc.).

10. **Use actual final product screenshots where required** (especially Days 3, 4, 6, 7 and listing media).

11. **Keep preliminary-estimate disclaimers** wherever budget ranges or planner outputs are mentioned.

12. **Preserve the central campaign narrative:**  
    *From vague enquiries to build-ready leads* — lead-generation and project-qualification system for builders.

13. **Keep Etsy as the only marketplace.**  
    Do not add Creem, Gumroad, Payhip, Shopify, WooCommerce, or other storefronts.

14. **Keep Udemy marked as future manual work.**  
    `UDEMY_COURSE_URL_PENDING` · manual creation and upload required · not published.

15. **Do not merge this branch into `main` from the second PC.**

16. **Commit frequently with descriptive messages.**

17. **Push only to `work/digital-marketing-strategy`.**  
    Never `git push --force` or `git push origin main` unless explicitly instructed by the human owner.

---

## Link rules

- Use `EXACT_ETSY_PRODUCT_URL_PENDING_PUBLICATION` until the listing is public.  
- Never substitute a **generic shop homepage** for the product listing.  
- Keep `PORTFOLIO_PROJECT_URL_PENDING`, `BLOG_URL_PENDING`, and related placeholders until real routes exist.

When both `handoff/grok/growth/` and `handoff/grok/campaign/` cover the same topic, edit **growth/** unless reconciling intentionally.
