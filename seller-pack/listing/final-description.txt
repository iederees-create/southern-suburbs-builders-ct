CORE DIFFERENTIATOR — THE RENOVATION SCOPE & BUDGET PLANNER
An interactive tool that helps visitors define their project — project type, dimensions or total floor area, number of rooms and storeys, finish level (Essential/Standard/Premium/Custom), scope of work (demolition, structural alterations, plumbing, electrical, waterproofing, roofing, painting, flooring), site access, occupancy, preferred start date, target budget, suburb and contact details — then generates:
• a measured project area
• a preliminary scope summary
• a configurable budget range
• a complexity indicator (Low/Medium/High)
• a trade checklist
• a list of pricing assumptions
• a missing-information checklist
• a photo checklist for the site visit
• a site-inspection recommendation
• a full structured project summary, ready to copy, print, or download

The summary can be handed off via WhatsApp or email — the exact message is always shown to the visitor first, in an on-screen confirmation dialog, and is only sent if they click "Confirm & Send." Nothing is sent automatically, and there is no backend or database — this is a purely front-end, static tool.

IMPORTANT: Planner output is PRELIMINARY GUIDANCE ONLY. It is NOT a binding quotation, fixed price, or formal tender. Actual pricing depends on final drawings, material selections, labour availability, regulatory approvals, and site conditions confirmed during a site inspection.
